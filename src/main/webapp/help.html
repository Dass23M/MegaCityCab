System Usage Guidelines

Explore how to make the most of our cab service with these simple steps.

  1. Visit the Login Page.
  2. Enter your username and password.
  3. Hit the Login button to access your account.
  4. New here? Click Register to get started!

  1. Log in and head to the Booking Page.
  2. Pick your pickup and drop-off spots.
  3. Select passengers and your preferred ride.
  4. Choose a driver from our trusted list.
  5. Click Confirm Booking—and you’re set!

  1. Navigate to the Bookings Page.
  2. Enter your booking number or customer ID.
  3. Press Search to see all the details.

  1. Post-booking, visit the Billing Page.
  2. Input your booking number.
  3. See your fare breakdown, including taxes and discounts.
  4. Click Print Bill for a hard copy.

Q: Forgot your password?

A: Head to Reset Password and follow the steps.

Q: Can I cancel a ride?

A: Yes! Visit Bookings Page and select Cancel Booking.
