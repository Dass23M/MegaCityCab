System Usage Guidelines

Welcome to Mega City Cab! This guide will help you navigate and use our system effectively.

  1. Go to the Login Page.
  2. Enter your username and password.
  3. Click the Login button.
  4. If you don't have an account, click Register to create one.

  1. After logging in, go to the Booking Page.
  2. Select your pickup and drop-off locations.
  3. Choose the number of passengers and a vehicle.
  4. Select a driver from the available list.
  5. Click Confirm Booking to complete your booking.

  1. Go to the Bookings Page.
  2. Enter your booking number or customer ID.
  3. Click Search to view your booking details.

  1. After confirming your booking, go to the Billing Page.
  2. Enter your booking number.
  3. The system will calculate the total fare, including taxes and discounts.
  4. Click Print Bill to generate a printable bill.

Q: How do I reset my password?

A: Go to the Reset Password page and follow the instructions.

Q: Can I cancel a booking?

A: Yes, go to the Bookings Page and click Cancel Booking.
